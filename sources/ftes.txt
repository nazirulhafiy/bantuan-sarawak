Skim Pendidikan Tinggi Percuma Sarawak (FTES)

What is Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES)?  
Apakah Skim Pendidikan Tinggi Percuma Sarawak (FTES)?

The Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES) is a Sarawak Government initiative that provides free tuition fees for eligible Sarawakian students pursuing approved Bachelor's Degree programmes starting in 2026.

This Scheme also covers an annual stipend of RM 15,000 per year to students from households with a monthly per capita income of RM 1,500 or less.

Skim Pendidikan Tinggi Percuma Sarawak (FTES) ialah inisiatif Kerajaan Sarawak yang menyediakan pembiayaan yuran pengajian kepada pelajar Sarawak yang mengikuti program Ijazah Sarjana Muda yang diluluskan mulai tahun 2026.

Skim ini turut merangkumi tambahan wang saku (stipend) sebanyak RM15,000 setahun untuk pelajar yang berasal daripada keluarga berpendapatan bulanan per kapita isi rumah RM1,500 dan ke bawah.

Which universities offer FTES?  
Universiti manakah yang menawarkan FTES?

FTES is available at the following Sarawak-owned Institutions of Higher Learning (SIHL):  
i. Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus, Kuching  
ii. Curtin University Malaysia, Miri  
iii. University of Technology Sarawak, Sibu  
iv. i-CATS University College, Kuching

FTES ditawarkan di Institusi Pengajian Tinggi Milik Sarawak (SIHL) berikut:  
i. Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus, Kuching  
ii. Curtin University Malaysia, Miri  
iii. University of Technology Sarawak, Sibu  
iv. i-CATS University College, Kuching

Who is eligible for FTES?  
Siapakah yang layak memohon FTES?

You are eligible for FTES if you:  
i. Are an Anak Sarawak holding a 'K' status identity card, or a Malaysian citizen with at least one parent holding a 'K' status identity card;  
ii. Are pursuing your first Bachelor's Degree;  
iii. Have received an offer to study in an approved Undergraduate Degree Programme at a Sarawak Institution of Higher Learning (SIHL);  
iv. Are enrolled as a full-time student; and  
v. Are not receiving any other scholarship or education sponsorship.

Anda layak memohon FTES jika:  
i. Merupakan Anak Sarawak yang memiliki kad pengenalan berstatus 'K', atau warganegara Malaysia yang mempunyai sekurang-kurangnya seorang ibu atau bapa berstatus 'K';  
ii. Sedang mengikuti Ijazah Sarjana Muda yang pertama;  
iii. Telah menerima tawaran ke program yang diluluskan di SIHL;  
iv. Berstatus pelajar sepenuh masa; dan  
v. Tidak menerima sebarang biasiswa atau tajaan pendidikan lain.

Does FTES cover Master's Degree and PhD programmes?  
Adakah FTES meliputi program Sarjana dan PhD?

No. FTES only covers full-time Bachelor's Degree programmes.  
Tidak. FTES hanya merangkumi program Ijazah Sarjana Muda sepenuh masa.

I am currently studying at Foundation or Diploma level. Am I eligible for FTES?  
Saya sedang mengikuti program Asasi atau Diploma. Adakah saya layak menerima FTES?

No. FTES only covers full-time Bachelor's Degree programmes.  
Tidak. FTES hanya merangkumi program Ijazah Sarjana Muda sepenuh masa.

Is FTES only for students from B40 families?  
Adakah FTES hanya untuk pelajar daripada keluarga B40?

No. FTES is available to all eligible Sarawakian students regardless of household income.  
However, students from households with a monthly per capita income of RM1,500 or below are eligible to receive an annual stipend of RM15,000, consisting of:  
i. RM5,000 for accommodation; and  
ii. RM10,000 for living expenses.

Tidak. FTES terbuka kepada semua pelajar Sarawak yang layak tanpa mengira status pendapatan keluarga.  
Walau bagaimanapun, pelajar daripada isi rumah dengan pendapatan per kapita bulanan RM1,500 dan ke bawah layak menerima stipend tahunan sebanyak RM15,000 yang terdiri daripada:  
i. RM5,000 bagi penginapan; dan  
ii. RM10,000 bagi sara hidup.

Is there an age limit for FTES?  
Adakah FTES mempunyai had umur?

No. There is currently no age limit under FTES. However, the Scheme only open to a student's first Bachelor's Degree.

Tidak. Pada masa ini, FTES tidak menetapkan had umur. Walau bagaimanapun, skim ini hanya terbuka untuk Ijazah Sarjana Muda pertama.

I am a part-time student. Am I eligible for FTES?  
Saya merupakan pelajar separuh masa. Adakah saya layak menerima FTES?

No. FTES is only available to full-time students.  
Tidak. FTES hanya terbuka kepada pelajar sepenuh masa.

I already have a Bachelor's Degree. Can I receive FTES for a second degree?  
Saya sudah mempunyai Ijazah Sarjana Muda. Adakah saya layak menerima FTES untuk ijazah kedua?

No. FTES is only available for students pursuing their first Bachelor's Degree.  
Tidak. FTES hanya terbuka kepada pelajar yang mengikuti Ijazah Sarjana Muda pertama.

I already have a scholarship/sponsorship. Can I still receive FTES?  
Saya sudah mempunyai biasiswa/tajaan. Adakah saya masih layak menerima FTES?

No. Students who are receiving a scholarship or sponsorship are not eligible for FTES.  
If you wish to apply for FTES, you must first withdraw from your existing scholarship or sponsorship. Any costs, obligations or consequences arising from the withdrawal are your responsibility.

Tidak. Pelajar yang sedang menerima biasiswa atau tajaan tidak layak menerima FTES.  
Sekiranya anda ingin memohon FTES, anda perlu menamatkan biasiswa atau tajaan sedia ada terlebih dahulu. Sebarang kos, obligasi atau implikasi akibat penamatan tersebut adalah di bawah tanggungjawab anda sendiri.

How do I apply for FTES?  
Bagaimanakah cara memohon FTES?

Applications must be submitted through the respective Sarawak-owned Institutions of Higher Learning (SIHL) where you intend to study.

Permohonan hendaklah dibuat melalui institusi pengajian tinggi milik Sarawak (SIHL) yang dipilih.

When can I apply for FTES?  
Bilakah saya boleh memohon FTES?

Applications for FTES are open according to the student intake schedule set by the respective SIHL.

Permohonan FTES dibuka mengikut jadual pengambilan pelajar yang ditetapkan oleh SIHL masing-masing.

Do I need to apply separately for the stipend?  
Adakah saya perlu memohon stipend secara berasingan?

No. Students from family with households with a monthly per capita income of RM 1,500 or less who are eligible for FTES will be automatically eligible for stipend without the need to apply for it separately.

Tidak. Pelajar daripada keluarga berpendapatan per kapita isi rumah RM1,500 dan ke bawah yang layak menerima FTES akan secara automatik layak untuk stipend tanpa perlu mengemukakan permohonan berasingan.

What is stipend?  
Apakah itu stipend?

Stipend is an additional annual allowance of RM15,000 provided to FTES students from households with a monthly per capita income of RM1,500 or below. The stipend comprises of:  
· RM5,000 for accommodation; and  
· RM10,000 for living expenses.

Stipend adalah wang saku tambahan sebanyak RM15,000 setahun untuk pelajar FTES yang berasal daripada keluarga berpendapatan bulanan per kapita isi rumah RM1,500 dan ke bawah. Stipend ini merangkumi:  
· RM5,000 untuk penginapan; dan  
· RM10,000 untuk sara hidup.

Who is eligible for the stipend?  
Siapakah yang layak menerima stipend?

You are eligible for stipend if you are:  
i. Eligible for FTES  
ii. Students from households with a monthly per capita income of RM1,500 or below based on the formula below:

Per capita income = Household Monthly Gross Income / Number of Household Members

Anda layak menerima stipend sekiranya:  
i. Layak menerima FTES  
ii. Berasal daripada keluarga berpendapatan bulanan per kapita isi rumah RM1,500 dan ke bawah berdasarkan formula berikut:

Pendapatan Per Kapita = Pendapatan Kasar Bulanan Isi Rumah / Bilangan Ahli Isi Rumah

Note: Formula shown as a fraction/division layout on the page; slash inserted for plain text. Denominator confirmed visually as “Number of Household Members” / “Bilangan Ahli Isi Rumah” (OCR garbled denominator on some crops).

How will the accommodation stipend be paid?
Bagaimanakah stipend penginapan disalurkan?

The accommodation stipend will be disbursed to and managed by the university (SIHL).
Stipend penginapan akan dibayar kepada dan diuruskan oleh pihak universiti (SIHL).

04
How will the living expenses stipend be paid?
Bagaimanakah stipend sara hidup disalurkan?

The living expenses stipend will be credited directly into the student's SPay Global account. Students are advised to regularly check their SPay Global account for notifications and to complete any required acknowledgement to ensure successful disbursement.
Stipend sara hidup akan dikreditkan terus ke akaun SPay Global pelajar. Pelajar dinasihatkan supaya sentiasa menyemak akaun SPay Global masing-masing dan melengkapkan pengesahan yang diperlukan bagi memastikan bantuan dapat disalurkan dengan lancar.

01
I am currently a second-year student in an approved programme at a SIHL. Am I eligible for FTES?
Saya merupakan pelajar tahun kedua, sedang menuntut dalam salah satu bidang yang tersenarai dalam FTES di salah sebuah institusi pengajian tinggi milik Sarawak (SIHL). Adakah saya masih layak untuk FTES?

Yes. FTES is eligible for new enrolments and all existing enrolments for approved programmes at Sarawak-owned Institutions of Higher Learning (SIHL) from 2026 onwards.
Ya. FTES terbuka untuk pelajar baru dan pelajar sedia ada yang mengikuti program yang diluluskan di Institusi Pengajian Tinggi Milik Sarawak (SIHL) bermula daripada tahun 2026.

02
Can I transfer to another university after receiving FTES?
Bolehkah saya berpindah ke universiti lain selepas menerima FTES?

No. FTES beneficiaries are not allowed to transfer between Sarawak-owned Institutions of Higher Learning (SIHL). Meanwhile, students from other institutions of higher learning who wish to transfer to SIHL under FTES programme, need to apply as new student. Once approved, the student will begin from Year 1 without any credit exemption.
Tidak. Penerima FTES tidak dibenarkan berpindah antara Institusi Pengajian Tinggi Milik Sarawak (SIHL). Manakala, bagi pelajar dari institusi pengajian tinggi lain yang ingin berpindah ke SIHL untuk menerima FTES, pelajar tersebut hendaklah memohon sebagai pelajar baru. Sekiranya permohonan diterima, pelajar tersebut hendaklah memulakan pengajian di SIHL bermula dari Tahun 1 tanpa pengecualian kredit.

03
Can I change my programme after receiving FTES?
Bolehkah saya menukar program pengajian selepas menerima FTES?

Yes. A change of programme is allowed once only and must be within the same SIHL.
Ya. Pertukaran program pengajian dibenarkan sekali sahaja dan mestilah dalam SIHL yang sama.

04
Can I defer my studies while receiving FTES?
Bolehkah saya menangguhkan pengajian semasa menerima FTES?

Yes. Students may apply for deferment for valid reasons. The maximum deferment period allowed is two semesters.
Ya. Pelajar boleh memohon penangguhan pengajian atas sebab yang munasabah. Tempoh penangguhan maksimum yang dibenarkan ialah dua semester.

05
What happen if I fail a subject and need to extend my study period?
Apakah yang berlaku sekiranya saya gagal sesuatu subjek dan perlu memanjangkan tempoh pengajian?

Any additional tuition fees or costs arising from an extension of study beyond the approved FTES duration must be borne by the student.
Sebarang kos atau yuran tambahan akibat pemanjangan tempoh pengajian melebihi tempoh yang diluluskan di bawah FTES hendaklah ditanggung oleh pelajar sendiri.

06
What happens to my FTES and stipend if I withdraw from my studies?
Apakah yang berlaku kepada FTES dan stipend saya sekiranya saya menarik diri daripada pengajian?

Your FTES assistance, including the stipend, will be terminated if you withdraw from your studies.
Bantuan FTES, termasuk stipend, akan ditamatkan sekiranya anda menarik diri daripada pengajian.

07
What happens to my FTES and stipend if I am suspended or dismissed by the university?
Apakah yang berlaku kepada FTES dan stipend saya sekiranya saya digantung atau diberhentikan oleh universiti?

Your FTES assistance, including the stipend, will be suspended during the suspension period. If you are dismissed from the university, your FTES assistance will be terminated.
Bantuan FTES, termasuk stipend, akan digantung sepanjang tempoh penggantungan pengajian. Sekiranya anda diberhentikan oleh universiti, bantuan FTES akan ditamatkan.

08
Can I apply for FTES again if I failed my previous studies?
Bolehkah saya memohon semula FTES sekiranya saya gagal dalam pengajian terdahulu?

No. FTES is awarded on a one-time basis only. Students who have previously received FTES are not eligible to apply again.
Tidak. FTES hanya diberikan sekali sahaja. Pelajar yang pernah menerima FTES tidak layak memohon semula.

09
I am an existing student who is eligible for FTES. Can I claim reimbursement for tuition fees that I have already paid for previous semesters?
Saya merupakan pelajar sedia ada yang layak menerima FTES. Bolehkah saya menuntut bayaran balik yuran pengajian yang telah saya bayar bagi semester terdahulu?

No. FTES only covers tuition fees for the period of study from 2026 onwards. Tuition fees paid for semesters before 2026 are not eligible for reimbursement under FTES.
Tidak. FTES hanya meliputi yuran pengajian bagi tempoh pengajian bermula tahun 2026 dan seterusnya. Yuran pengajian yang telah dibayar bagi semester sebelum tahun 2026 tidak layak dituntut sebagai bayaran balik di bawah FTES.
