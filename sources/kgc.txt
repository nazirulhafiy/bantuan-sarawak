Activate Kenyalang Gold Card (KGC)
Share
Introduction
The Kenyalang Gold Card (KGC) is an initiative introduced by the Sarawak Government to provide social support and recognition to Sarawakians aged 60 years and above and hold a MyKad with status ‘K’ .
KGC enables senior citizens to access various privileges, including discounts on a range of products and services offered by participating merchants.
Iin addition to compassionate assistance amounting to RM1,800 to partially defray funeral expenses, subject to specified terms and conditions.
Who's eligible?
1. Malaysian citizen with status “K” on MyKad and;
2. Must be age 60 and above.
How to get the service?
Individuals who meet the eligibility criteria will automatically be granted entitlement to receive KGC. You only need to activate it online, after which the virtual card can be accessed through the SarawakPass mobile application.
For any enquiries or assistance regarding KGC, you may visit any Social Welfare Department of Sarawak office, call us at 082-374888, or email us at kgc@sarawak.gov.my. Alternatively, you may contact our Talikhidmat Centre by completing the online enquiry form, calling +6082-555999, or emailing 555999@sarawak.gov.my.
