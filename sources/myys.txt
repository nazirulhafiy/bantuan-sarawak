Free School Uniform 2024 ×
Important Notice (1):
This program aims to help ease the financial burden on parents when it comes to providing school necessities for their children . Each recipient will receive RM200 worth of e-vouchers redeemable for school uniforms and other schooling needs at registered vendors statewide. The schools will identify eligible students based on the eligibility criteria and submit the names to the District Office (Pejabat Daerah) through the District Education Office (PPD):
Eligibility Criteria:
1. Students in Primary 1 and Form 1 only.
2. Registered in e-Kasih database. 3. Also for students with special needs (OKU) and Penan students.
4. Verified by the District Focus Group.
