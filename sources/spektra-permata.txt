Rumah Spektra Permata (RSP)
The Rumah Spektra Permata (RSP) is a housing program by the Sarawak State Government that helps low—and middle-income families (B40 and M40) own a home. The Chief Minister of Sarawak launched it on 15 February 2017 at the Kampung Extension Scheme in Lundu.
The program prioritizes landowners under the Kampung Extension Scheme (SPK) and Kampung Resettlement Scheme (SPS), as the Land and Survey Department allocates.
ELIGIBILTY CRITERIA
Lot holders (allottees) allocated by the Land and Survey Department.
Applicants must be between 18 and 65 years old (Joint financing for applicants aged 50  and above)
Must not own a house or land
Married or a single parent
Household income between RM 1,500 and RM 6,000
Loan financing through Mutiara Mortgage & Credit Sdn Bhd
HOUSE INFORMATION & SPECIFICATIONS
Stilt House, Single-Storey Detached/ Single-Storey Terrace and Double-Storey Detached/ Double-Storey Terrace
Built-up area between 750 and 850 square feet
3 bedrooms, a living room, dining area, and two(2) bathrooms
Built using the Industrialized Building System (IBS) for quality and durability
Estimated selling price: Below RM200,000
