Sarawak Micro Credit Scheme (SMCS)
Share
Introduction
Sarawak Micro Credit Scheme (SMCS) is a financial assistance in terms of soft loan to the B40 entrepreneurs, small and micro enterprises. This scheme is open to sarawakians (bumiputera and non-bumiputera) who reside and operate their business in Sarawak in all business sectors except agriculture. This financial assistance is only applicable for working capital, renovation of business premises and purchasing of machinery and equipment.
What are the type of scheme available?
There are two type of Sarawak Micro Credit Scheme as follow:
Type  1:
Loan/Financing Amount: RM1,000.00 - RM10,000.00
Applicable for B40 entrepreneurs only
Zero interest/profit
Minimum Requirement: Business Permits issued by Councils or relevant authorities
Loan/Financing Tenure: 1-3 years
Type 2:
Loan/Financing Amount: RM5,001.00 - RM50,000.00
Applicable for B40 entrepreneurs, micro and small enterprises
4% interest/profit
Minimum Requirement: Business Registration from District Office or LHDN or SSM and relevant licences
Loan/Financing Tenure: 1-5 year
Who's eligible?
Sarawakian (Bumiputera or Non-Bumiputera)
Resides and operates business in Sarawak
Has operated business for at least 6 months
Open to all business sector except agriculture
What you'll need?
A copy of valid business permit or business registration or business license with relevant  authorities
A copy of MyKads  of all the directors / owners / partners
Bank statements for the last 6 months
Other supporting documents as required by bank
Payment / Charges
No charges
How to get the service?
The Sarawak Micro Credit Scheme (SMCS) can be applied through the following channels:
Online
Over the counter at Sarawak Economic Development Corporation (SEDC) or Agrobank
For any enquiries or need help, you can reach us through our contact number +6082-443339 or email at mintred@sarawak.gov.my
