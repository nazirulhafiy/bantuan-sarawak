Housing Deposit Assistance Scheme (HDAS)
CM : HDAS will help B40, M40 group purchase first home while boosting local economy
Abang Johari signs the plaque to launch the scheme. From left are Penguang and Dr Sim. — Bernama photo
The Housing Deposit Assistance Scheme (HDAS) is offered to the B40 and M40 groups in Sarawak to help them own their own homes. Through this unique scheme, the State Government will channel deposit assistance of 10% or a maximum of RM10,000 for affordable housing purchase deposits to first home buyers.
HDAS is also extended to landowners who want to build houses on their land. Land ownership must be land allocated by the Land and Survey Department under the Village Expansion Scheme (SPK) and the Resettlement Scheme (SPS).
For this process, participants can contact MUTIARA at 082-313035 or apply directly at the MUTIARA office.
WHO IS ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE THIS HDAS INITIATIVE?
1) FIRST HOUSE Ownership for Sarawakians of B40 & M40 group
2) BUY AN AFFORDABLE HOME from a registered developer or;
3) BUILD A HOUSE on their land through the Village Expansion Scheme (SPK)/Resettlement Scheme (SPS) Program
