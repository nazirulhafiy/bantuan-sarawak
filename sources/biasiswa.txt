Scholarship
Sarawak Foundation Tun Taib Scholarship (BYSTT)
Objective
Providing scholarships to outstanding students pursuing studies at any institution of higher learning locally or abroad, with priority given to Private Higher Education Institutions owned by the Sarawak State Government.
Priority is given to courses in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) and selected fields , as listed below:
1) Engineering (priority for Aerospace Engineering)
2) Information Technology (priority for Semiconductor Technology)
3) Medicine and Health
4) Life Sciences
5) Food Technology
6) Psychology and Counselling
7) Construction and Land Development
8) Accounting
9) Finance
10) Law
Requirement
Sarawakian (at least one parent must be of Sarawakian origin).
Malaysian citizen.
Must obtain a credit in Bahasa Melayu in the SPM examination or its equivalent, as follows::
1) A minimum Grade C in Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM).
2) A minimum Grade B6 in the Unified Examination Certificate (UEC).
Achieved a minimum CGPA/PNGK of 3.00 for those with qualifications such as STPM / Matriculation / Foundation / Diploma or equivalent.
Undergraduate students currently pursuing studies at local IPTA or IPTS must have a minimum CGPA/PNGK of 3.00.
Postgraduate students (Master’s, PhD & Specialisation) pursuing studies locally or abroad must have achieved excellent academic results (CGPA/PNGK 3.00 and above) during their undergraduate or Master’s studies.
Full Time Study Mode.
Pass the shortlist screening and interview session.
Application
Coursework:
YBSTAR online application.
Applications are opened once a year through advertisements and will be published on the Sarawak Foundation Website, Sarawak Foundation Official Social Media and Local Newspapers.
First degree studies: Domestic only.
Masters and Ph.D Studies: Domestic and Abroad.
Research:
Applications are open throughout the year.
Masters and PhD studies at home and abroad.
The application form can be downloaded from the Sarawak Foundation website.
Please attach the ‘Research Proposal’ that has been approved.
Elements of Scholarship Payment
Tuition Fees, Living Allowance, Book Allowance, Equipment Allowance, Internship Allowance & Thesis Allowance.
Return flight ticket (once a year for studies in the country / once during studies abroad).
Sarawak Tunku Abdul Rahman Scholarship Foundation (YBSTAR)
Objective
Providing scholarships to outstanding students who are studying at Public Higher Education Institutions (IPTA) or Local Private Higher Education Institutions (IPTS) in NON-STEM fields (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics).
Priority is given to IPTS owned by the Sarawak Government.
Requirement
Sarawakian (at least one of the parents is a Sarawakian).
Malaysian citizens.
It is mandatory to obtain a credit in the Bahasa Melayu subject in the SPM examination or an equivalent examination as follows:
1. Obtained at least a Grade C for the Malaysian Certificate of Education (SPM).
2. Obtained at least a Grade C for the General Certificate of Ordinary Level (GCE O-Level).
3. Obtained at least a Grade B6 for the Unified Examination Certificate (UEC).
First degree students currently studying at IPTA or local IPTS with CGPN / PNGK 3.0 and above.
Obtain CGPN / PNGK 3.00 and above for graduates of Sijil Tinggi Pelajaran Malaysia (STPM) or its equivalent / Diploma / Matriculation / Foundation and its equivalent.
Postgraduate students (Master, Ph.D & Expertise) who are studying at IPT locally and abroad.
Achieve excellent academic achievement (CGPA / PNGK 3.00 and above) during the First or Master’s degree.
Full Time Study Mode.
Pass the shortlist and interview session.
Application
Coursework & Research:
YBSTAR online application.
Applications are opened once a year through advertisements and will be published on the Sarawak Foundation Website, Sarawak Foundation Official Social Media and Local Newspapers.
First degree studies: In the country only.
Masters and Ph.D Studies: Locally and Abroad.
Elements of Scholarship Payment
Tuition Fees, Living Allowance, Book Allowance, Equipment Allowance, Internship Allowance & Thesis Allowance.
Return flight ticket (once a year for studies in the country / once during studies abroad).
UNIMAS Special Medical Program Scholarship
Objective
Specifically for students pursuing a Bachelor of Medicine Program at Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (UNIMAS).
Every year 40 students will be selected as an effort to increase the number of doctors in Sarawak.
Requirement
Sarawakian (at least one of the parents is a Sarawakian).
Malaysian.
First degree students currently studying at UNIMAS with a GPA / CGPA of 3.00 and above.
Obtained an offer and confirmation of studying Medicine Program at Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (UNIMAS).
Application
Yayasan Sarawak will receive a list of eligible students from Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (UNIMAS).
Pass the shortlist screening and interview session.
Elements of Scholarship Payment
Tuition Fee & Accommodation Fee.
Subsistence Allowance, Book Allowance, Tool Allowance, Practical Training Allowance & Thesis Allowance.
Round trip flight tickets (once a year for studies in the country / Once during studies abroad).
Sarawak Foundation Local Scholarship (BTYS)
Objective
Providing financial assistance to underprivileged students at the National Secondary School level in Sarawak.
Funding the students to continue schooling as well as encouraging the improvement of students’ academic performance.
Requirement
Sarawakian (at least one of the parents is a Sarawakian).
Malaysian citizen.
Open to National Secondary School students in Sarawak, and priority is given to Form 2 students.
The screening of student selection is based on family income, academic achievement and co-curricular involvement that has been confirmed by the school.
Students are NOT recipients of scholarships or financial aid from the government or external parties.
Students do not have discipline problems at school.
Assistance
The fixed rate for Form 2 to Form 5 is RM800.00 per year for each student.
Application
Applications are filled out online by the school through the My-Yayasan application.
Admission Assistance to Tertiary / Technical Education
Objective
Helping to finance the initial cost of students starting their studies at Public Higher Education Institutions and IPTS owned by the Sarawak State Government.
Requirement
Sarawakian (at least one of the parents is a Sarawakian).
Malaysian citizen.
It is mandatory to obtain a credit in the Bahasa Melayu subject in the SPM examination or an equivalent examination as follows:
1. Obtained at least a Grade C for the Malaysian Certificate of Education (SPM).
2. Obtained at least a Grade C for the General Certificate of Ordinary Level (GCE O-Level).
3. Obtained at least a Grade B6 for the Unified Examination Certificate (UEC).
Open to students for semester one (1) only.
Pursuing Full Time study mode.
Following studies at the government sponsored Skill / Technical Training Institution, Public Higher Education Institution, Swinburne University of Technology, Kuching; Sarawak University of Technology, Sibu; Curtin University Malaysia, Miri; Laila Taib Sibu College and i-CATS University College, Kuching.
The parents or guardians of students have a per capita income of RM1,500.00 and below.
Only covers entry level Certificate / Matriculation / Foundation / Pre Diploma / Diploma / Bachelor’s degree.
Assistance
These non-repayable grants are as follows:
In Sarawak : RM1,000.00
Outside Sarawak : RM1,500.00
Application
Open throughout the year.

BP40 Bursary
Objective
Bursary BP40 Yaysan Sarawak provides opportunities and helps students from low or medium income families (B40 / M40) to continue their studies at Foundation Level, Diploma and First Degree with Subsidized Tuition Fees Covering 80% of the total tuition fees.
Requirement
Open to students from B40 and M40 families.
Malaysian.
It is mandatory to obtain a credit in the Bahasa Melayu subject in the SPM examination or an equivalent examination as follows:
1. Obtained at least a Grade C for the Malaysian Certificate of Education (SPM).
2. Obtained at least a Grade C for the General Certificate of Ordinary Level (GCE O-Level.
3. Obtained at least a Grade B6 for the Unified Examination Certificate (UEC).
Sarawakian (where one of the parents is Sarawakian Bumpitera). At Swinburne, Curtin, UTS, KLT and i-CATS is limited to 100 Bumiputera students for each university each year.
For Non-Bumiputera Sarawakian: Only at UTS, and limited to 100 non-Bumiputera students each year.
Applications are made through institutions – Swinburne, Curtin, UTS, KLT and i CATS.
Applicants are also (i) Eligible to apply for Yayasan Sarawak Local Scholarship Loans to cover the remaining fees that still need to be paid and (ii) eligible to apply for Admission Assistance to Tertiary / Technical Education.
Open throughout the year.
Required documents
Letter of offer to study at the relevant institution.
MyKad – Students, parents and guardians and siblings who are still dependent on their parents.
Student’s birth certificate.
SPM results (Must have credits in Bahasa Melayu).
Latest salary statement of parent / guardian OR pension statement if retired) OR confirmation letter that parents are not employed.
Bank Statement (make sure the bank account is still Active).
Sarawak Returning Graduate Initiative (i-GPS)
Objective
The purpose of subsidizing flights from Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah or Labuan back to Sarawak.
A maximum of two (2) applications are approved in one year.
Maximum claim of RM300 for each application / trip.
Requirement
Sarawakian (at least one of the parents is a Sarawakian).
Malaysian citizen.
Aged 18 years and above; still active in full-time studies at any Higher Institution in Malaysia (outside Sarawak) when submitting the application / flight.
Flights are not limited to certain dates in a year.
Applications are open throughout the year.
Please submit a complete application within 60 days after the date of flight.
Yayasan Sarawak online application .
All supporting documents MUST be uploaded into the application system.
For any inquiry regarding the application, please contact Sarawak Volunteers at 082-443265 or email sarawakvolunteers@gmail.com
Required documents
Confirmation letter of active study status at the latest higher institution.
Bank statement (make sure the bank account is still active).
Flight boarding pass.
Flight itinerary.
