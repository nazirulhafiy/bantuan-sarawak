Apply for Bantuan Ibu Bersalin (BIB)
Share
Introduction
Bantuan Ibu Bersalin (BIB) is an initiative by the Sarawak Government that provides a cash grant of RM450.00 to every mother who gives birth to a Sarawak-born child. The assistance aims to support mothers’ needs, such as postnatal care and healthy nutrition during the confinement period. The initiative was introduced in 2020 and is open to all mothers regardless of race, religion or socioeconomic status.
What you'll need?
An active and verified SarawakPass (formerly known as SarawakID) account
An active SPayGlobal account
A copy of the applicant’s Identity Card (front and back) or passport biopage
A copy of the husband’s/guardian’s Identity Card (front and back) or passport biopage
A copy of the Marriage Certificate if the applicant does not hold ‘K’ status
A copy of the Maternal Health Record Book (front page)
A copy of the Doctor’s Confirmation / Hospital Discharge Note / Baby’s Birth Certificate.
How to get the service?
Online application through the iSarawakCare portal
Who's eligible?
A Malaysian mother with 'K' (Sarawak) resident status, or the wife of a Malaysian citizen with 'K' (Sarawak) resident status
Has given birth to a child either in or outside Sarawak
Has delivered at 22 weeks of gestation or above.
The application must be submitted within three (3) months after the date of delivery. For cases of stillbirth or miscarriage, applications may be made once the pregnancy has reached 22 weeks of gestation or above.
Payment / Charges
Free of charge
For enquiries or status checks regarding BIB, please contact the BIB Secretariat through:
(i) Office line: 082-374700;
(ii) WhatsApp line: 014-6881398 (operates during office hours);
(iii) Email: bib@sarawak.gov.my; or
(iv) Check the status under the Timeline section in iSarawakCare.
